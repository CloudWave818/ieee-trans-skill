# Scientific Logic Audit

Audit the manuscript-level causal and argumentative chain.

## Checks

- Is the primary scientific question falsifiable and condition-bounded?
- Does the cited limitation describe the closest relevant approaches rather than a generic field problem?
- Is the research gap distinct from “method X has not been used”?
- Does every challenge prevent a trivial solution?
- Does the core insight logically motivate the mechanism?
- Does each mechanism answer a stated challenge?
- Does each major claim follow from the mechanism under explicit assumptions?
- Does each claim point to evidence capable of testing it?
- Are theory-led and system-led burdens separated rather than averaged?
- Are limitations and boundary conditions visible?
- Do section transitions advance the argument rather than merely change topics?
- Do abstract and conclusion stay within the body evidence?

## Blockers

Mark `BLOCKER` for a missing central gap, a mechanism unrelated to the stated problem, circular novelty logic, mutually inconsistent assumptions, or a central claim with no possible evidence path.

## Output

Record the broken link, evidence location, downstream claims, required repair, and re-audit trigger.
