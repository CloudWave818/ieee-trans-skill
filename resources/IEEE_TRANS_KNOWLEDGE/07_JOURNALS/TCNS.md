## Scientific-argument tendency

- Treat the paper's primary research type as the first routing key; the venue/domain profile refines it rather than replacing it.
- Connect each limitation to a mechanism and then to a matching test or proof. Evidence layers that are uncommon here remain optional, not forbidden.
- Use the median and IQR as planning anchors, never as page or figure quotas.

## Differentiators and non-generalizable rules

- A tendency is local when it is concentrated in this scope or supported by a small eligible sample. It must not be promoted to a General Transactions rule without cross-journal evidence.
- A missing automated signal is a review target. It is not a basis for claiming that the paper omitted the element.
