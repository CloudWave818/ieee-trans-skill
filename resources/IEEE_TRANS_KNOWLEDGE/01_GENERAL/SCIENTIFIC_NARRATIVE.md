## Actionable use

- State the operating condition that makes the problem difficult before presenting modules.
- Map every major limitation to a design mechanism and every mechanism claim to a proof or experiment.
- End result paragraphs with a bounded scientific implication, not an adjective.

## Applicability limits

- Do not infer author intent from an undetected keyword; source inspection remains necessary.
