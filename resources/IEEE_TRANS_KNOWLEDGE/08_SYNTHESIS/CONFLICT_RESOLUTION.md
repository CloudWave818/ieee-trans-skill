# Conflict Resolution

## 1. THEORY_VS_SYSTEM

Conflict: Theory-led control papers prioritise assumptions/proofs; robotics and industrial system papers prioritise implementation and physical evidence.

Resolution: Route by primary paper type; do not average proof and deployment burdens.

## 2. PHYSICAL_VALIDATION

Conflict: Physical experiments are common evidence in some system domains but not a universal expectation for formal control contributions.

Resolution: Require physical evidence only when the claim reaches real-world feasibility or the target profile shows a strong system tendency.

## 3. ABLATION_VS_PROOF

Conflict: Ablation is natural for modular learning methods, whereas theorem-led mechanisms may be isolated by proofs and controlled comparisons.

Resolution: Choose the evidence form that directly identifies the claimed mechanism.

## 4. SECTION_SPLIT

Conflict: Problem Formulation, Preliminaries, and Related Work can be independent or integrated.

Resolution: Split when the section creates a reusable contract; merge when separation fragments the argument.

## 5. FIGURE_DENSITY

Conflict: Trajectory/system papers need visual evidence; proof-centric papers may use few figures.

Resolution: Condition visual plans on claim type and paper type, not the corpus-wide median alone.

Conflicts are routing decisions, not averaged compromises.
