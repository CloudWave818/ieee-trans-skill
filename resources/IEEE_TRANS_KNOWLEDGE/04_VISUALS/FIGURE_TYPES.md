## Actionable use

- Choose among motivation/problem, framework, architecture, scenario/trajectory, quantitative, robustness/generalization, runtime, real-world, or failure roles.
- Do not force every paper to use every role.

- Type labels come from captions and may miss visual functions not named in text.
