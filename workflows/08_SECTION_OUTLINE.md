# 08 Section Outline and Paper Blueprint

## Entry

Require research architecture, contributions, claim–evidence matrix, experiment plan, visual plan, and page budget for full-paper work.

## Procedure

1. Copy `templates/PAPER_BLUEPRINT.md`.
2. Choose sections by scientific function; split Problem Formulation, Preliminaries, or Related Work only when the section creates a reusable contract.
3. For every section and subsection, record purpose, scientific question, required content/evidence, figures, tables, equations, input dependencies, expected length, and both transitions.
4. Map every contribution and major claim to at least one section and evidence location.
5. Verify that assumptions appear before use, mechanisms before effects, and evidence after the relevant setup.
6. Prevent the Introduction from absorbing a literature catalogue or results discussion.
7. Place Abstract and Title last in the production order even though they appear first in the manuscript.

## Coherence checks

- No section exists only because a template usually has it.
- No contribution appears first in the Conclusion.
- No figure/table precedes the setup needed to interpret it.
- No results claim lacks an experiment or data dependency.
- Adjacent sections have explicit logical transitions, not topic-name transitions.

## Exit

The blueprint is complete enough that each section can receive a Section Brief without rediscovering the paper's architecture. Without this output, do not generate a full manuscript.
