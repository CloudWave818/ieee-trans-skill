# 07 Page Budget

## Entry

Require paper type, locked or candidate journal, method complexity, experiment complexity, and planned assets.

## Procedure

1. Copy `templates/PAGE_BUDGET.md`.
2. Load `IEEE_TRANS_KNOWLEDGE/01_GENERAL/PAGE_AND_SPACE_BUDGET.md`, the relevant domain profiles, paper-type route, and target journal profile.
3. Treat observed medians/IQRs as descriptive anchors only.
4. Allocate argument space to Introduction, Related Work/Preliminaries, Problem Formulation, Method, Theory, Experiments, Results, Conclusion, figures, tables, and references.
5. Express estimates as ranges with a rationale and confidence.
6. Reserve space first for claim-bearing method/evidence, then compress background and duplicated explanation.
7. Reconcile visual spans with narrative space; do not count a full-width figure as free.

## Constraints

- Current official author instructions control format and limits; verify them when the task depends on exact rules.
- Do not turn `median 13.5 pages` or any corpus statistic into a requirement.
- Use `0` only for a deliberately omitted section, not unknown length.
- Preserve a contingency reserve for revisions and final formatting.

## Exit

The budget sums consistently, reflects journal/paper-type conditions, protects the main claims, and identifies sections that must shrink if the estimate is exceeded.
