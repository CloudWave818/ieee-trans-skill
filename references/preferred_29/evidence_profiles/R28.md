## Observed evidence order

Shared experimental protocol -> three task/scenes -> controlled geometric vs VLM selection across backbones -> capability coverage and targeted grounding/capability diagnostics (pp.5-7).

## Actual comparison roles

- Geometric Greedy and five VLM backbones in same robot stack; without SCA, text-only LLM grounding vs visual grounding diagnostics (pp.6-7).

## Actual metrics and aggregation

- All-target success, successful-trial wall-clock time, per-robot/high-level total steps, reductions; qualitative task diagnostics (pp.6-7).

## Controls and sampling

Three scenes and thirty trials/method. Same robot descriptions, perception/grounding, candidates, planning/tasks/initial poses; vary only target selection for greedy comparison. Time and steps condition on success (pp.6-7).

## Mechanism and stress tests

Survivor, home multi-target/door and hospital search; five backbones show reliability-efficiency trade-off; appearance/relational grasp and clock reading isolate semantics/grounding (pp.6-7).

## Compute and physical evidence

Unity/ROS1, C++ plus Python VLM interface, Ryzen7 9700X/~32 GB; two simulated UAVs and one mobile manipulator. Conclusion treats physical deployment as future work. Bandwidth/storage savings are explicitly not measured (p.5).

## Presentation to inherit

One aggregate mean+/-std table has reliability, per-robot attribution and reductions; scene sequence with first-person/minimap connects the decisions to task history. Capability checklist is separate (pp.6-7).

## Do not generalize

Motion/interaction in simulator is not real physical trials. Fewer decisions need not mean faster wall-clock time when backbone latency differs. Do not promote bounded memory into measured communication savings.
