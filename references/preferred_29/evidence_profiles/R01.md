## Controls and sampling

Prediction shares polynomial/history/horizon parameters; >15000 predictions per noise condition. Planner receives the same ground-truth future target motion, separating planner quality from differing predictors; 200 missions per target-speed scenario (p.6).

## Mechanism and stress tests

Vary observation Gaussian noise and target speed; indoor clutter and dense forest test re-location/tracking behavior. A distinct module ablation was not identified in pp.5-6.

## Compute and physical evidence

Broad-FOV three-camera detection; approximately 20 ms system total and 15 Hz replanning; indoor/outdoor maps paired with real scene images (p.5). CPU model not resolved from these pages.

## Presentation to inherit

## Do not generalize

An end-to-end system claim also needs end-to-end tests: supplying ground-truth target futures isolates the planner and cannot establish realistic prediction robustness. Do not treat lower acceleration alone as a measured energy result.
